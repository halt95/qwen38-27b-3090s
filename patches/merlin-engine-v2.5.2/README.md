Its notes and evidence as series patch 0012:

Change (community issue #2 / PR #3; revised after code review): **guard reader idle quiesce**: the bounds-guard reader (default `warn`) copied its counters every 0.05 s forever, which holds idle cards in P2. The worker wraps `execute_model`, `sample_tokens` and `execute_dummy_batch` in `guard_telemetry.engine_call` (call generation + open-call count under one lock; no device work on the engine thread). With no call open for a full interval the reader records a fence event on the engine stream (from the reader thread), keeps polling until it completes, then reads every counter once more (coherent: no producer running), reports, and parks on a condition variable with no device work until the next call. Hits from the last step before idle (MTP proposal, RecoverSSM commit graph, async-scheduled work) are reported and fatal sites armed before it parks. **Single-stream invariant enforced at run time**: every engine call and every eager guarded launch must run on the engine thread and a fenced stream, else the reader falls back to always-poll with an ERROR line. Fatal counts arm on the first copy that shows them (the maxima follow on the confirming copy); detection while serving is cadence-bound (armed ~1-2 intervals, reported ~2-3, raised at the next `execute_model`), not one step. The drain's waits are cancelled by a resumed call, a stop or a suspend. Lifecycle: shutdown joins the reader before the final drain and a later worker in the same process gets a fresh reader; sleep suspends the reader (one settled read, no reads until wake-up; a timed-out suspend makes sleep raise; the drain is refused while asleep). Falls back to always-poll without the worker wiring or with `VLLM_E2_GUARD_IDLE=0` (both serve scripts set it to 1). Tests `tests/test_0012_guard_idle_quiesce.py`
